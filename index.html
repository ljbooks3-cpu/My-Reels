This app is configured for your Google OAuth client. Your Client ID is stored in the app code; no Google password or secret is stored.
Google Drive is the shared library. Sync uploads new videos once, downloads new videos to other devices, and removes duplicates by SHA-256.

Storage

Build: v22-cross-device-sync • One Drive library shared across phone and laptop.
Local videos stay on this device. When Drive is connected, this app creates a private “My Reels” folder in your Drive and stores videos, covers, and a metadata file there.

⚠️ Important

Google Drive storage uses your own Drive quota. Do not clear browser/site data until your Drive backup has completed.
